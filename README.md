# ✒️ softInk 
## *Crafted with soul.*
*Where words find their homes.*

**softInk** is a minimalist poetry platform designed to express emotions through words. It is a digital space where poetry, thoughts, and creativity flow freely—soft like ink, deep like emotion.

🔗 Live Site: https://soft-ink.vercel.app/

---

## 🌙 What is this?
softInk is a digital library of poems -simple ,soulful and made for slow reading.No noise,no clutter.Just words that matter.
Browse by language.Pick a poem.Let it sit with you for a while.
The goal is to create a soft, modern, and expressive platform where words matter more than visuals.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- Typescript,Javacript,Node,Express and MonogDb.
- Used vercel and render for deployement.

## ✨ Features

- 📖A growing collection of original poems.
- 🌐Available in **English** and **hindi**.
- 🧠A calm,minimal reading experience.
- 📫A way to reach out and share thoughts.

## 😇The spirit Behind it
>*Ink.Paper.Soul*

Every poem here was written somewhere between memory and longing - about growing up,letting go ,and finding home in the smallest things.
